IT Guide: Passwords and Account Lockout

Password rules. Company passwords must have at least 14 characters and must be changed every 180 days. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords. Using a password manager is recommended; the company provides licenses for all employees through the Software Center.

Resetting a forgotten password. Go to the self-service portal at id.brightwave.example and choose Forgot password. Confirm your identity with the Authenticator app. The new password works on all systems after about 5 minutes. If you cannot use the Authenticator app, call the IT Service Desk on the internal number 4000 and be ready to verify your identity with your employee number and your manager's name.

Account lockout. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. It unlocks automatically; if you cannot wait, use the self-service portal option Unlock account.

Multi-factor authentication. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts. When you get a new phone, transfer the Authenticator app before you wipe the old phone. If you already wiped it, open a ticket in the IT Service Desk with the category Access and ask for an MFA reset; a new enrollment link is sent to your manager within 4 working hours.

Suspicious emails. If you receive an email asking for your password or a payment that looks unusual, do not click the links. Forward the message to security@brightwave.example and delete it.
