Brightwave Remote Work Policy

Hybrid model. Brightwave works in a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely on the other days. Teams can agree on a different schedule with their department director, but every team must meet in person at least twice a week.

Working from abroad. Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. The request must be approved by the manager and by the HR department at least 30 days in advance, because tax and social security rules differ between countries. Working from abroad without approval is a policy violation.

Home office equipment. Every employee gets a one-time home office budget of 1500 PLN, which can be spent on a desk, a chair or a monitor. The budget is renewed every 4 years. Keep the invoice and submit it through the expense system described in the Expense Policy. Laptops and headsets are provided by the IT department and are not paid from this budget.

Core hours. Regardless of location, everyone should be available between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time. Meetings should not be scheduled outside these hours without the consent of all participants.

Internet and electricity. Brightwave pays a monthly flat allowance of 100 PLN for remote work costs for employees working from home at least 8 days per month. The allowance is added automatically to the payroll.
