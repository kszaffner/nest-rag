Brightwave Expense Policy

What can be reimbursed. Brightwave reimburses business expenses that are necessary for work: business travel, accommodation, meals during trips, conference tickets, training and approved equipment. Personal expenses, fines and alcohol are never reimbursed.

How to submit an expense. Upload the receipt or invoice in the Expense app within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted later than 30 days after the purchase are rejected automatically. Each claim needs a short description and a project code. The invoice must be issued to Brightwave Sp. z o.o. with the company tax number, receipts without the tax number are accepted only for amounts below 100 PLN.

Approval limits. Expenses up to 500 PLN are approved by the direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5000 PLN require approval from the department director. Anything above 5000 PLN must be approved by the finance department before the purchase is made, not after.

Business travel. Flights must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is the default; business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval. The daily meal allowance on business trips is 45 EUR abroad and 40 PLN in Poland.

Payment. Approved expenses are paid together with the monthly salary if they were approved before the 20th day of the month; otherwise they are paid in the next month.
