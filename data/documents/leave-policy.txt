Brightwave Leave Policy

Annual leave. Every full-time employee at Brightwave receives 26 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees who join during the year receive leave proportionally to the number of full months they work. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they expire.

How to request leave. Submit the request in the HR portal at least 14 days before the planned start for leave longer than 5 working days, and at least 3 working days before for shorter leave. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. Leave is considered approved only after you receive the confirmation email from the HR portal.

On-demand leave. Each employee may take up to 4 days of on-demand leave per year, which can be requested on the same day by messaging the manager before 10:00. On-demand days are part of the 26 days, not an addition to them.

Sick leave. If you are ill, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Sick leave is paid at 80 percent of the salary and requires an electronic medical certificate from the second day onward. Do not use annual leave days for sick days.

Parental leave. Employees with a child under 14 are entitled to 2 additional paid days of care leave per year. Request them in the HR portal under the category Care leave and attach no documents.
